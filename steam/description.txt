[h1]New and Improved Chairs[/h1]

Every built chair becomes a heavy dwarven slat-back chair that you can turn to face north, east, south or west, and slide a few pixels inside its tile to pull it up to a table. The chair takes its colour from the material it is made of.

It is purely cosmetic. The game has no idea of chair facing, so nothing about the chair, the room or your fortress changes, and removing the mod puts every chair back as it was.

[h2]How to use it[/h2]
[list]
[*]Click a chair. After its name in the sheet there is a small round arrow: click it to turn the chair a quarter turn.
[*]Next to it are four small triangles. Each click moves the chair's picture one pixel that way. A triangle goes dim when the chair has reached the edge of its tile.
[*]A red x puts the chair back where it rests.
[/list]
Facings and positions are saved with your fortress.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[b]Requires DFHack (free on Steam), kept up to date. Without it this mod does nothing.[/b] Tested on:
[list]
[*]Dwarf Fortress 53.16 (Steam, on Windows under Wine)
[*]DFHack 53.16-r1.1
[/list]
The mod is a plain DFHack script. There is no compiled plugin, so it is not tied to one DFHack build. Native Windows, Linux and Proton are untested.

[h2]Installing[/h2]
[list]
[*]Subscribe and wait for Steam to finish downloading.
[*]Fully exit the game, then start it through DFHack and load your fortress.
[/list]
Existing saves work. Do not add the mod to a world's mod list at world generation; it does nothing there.

[h2]What the mod puts on your computer[/h2]
Nothing outside its own folder. DFHack runs scripts straight from a Workshop mod, so there is no install step. The mod is three files, used where they are:
[code]scripts_modinstalled/improved-chairs.lua
scripts_modinstalled/internal/improved-chairs/template.lua
scripts_modinstalled/internal/improved-chairs/chair_controls.png[/code]
The first is the script, the second the chair art, the third the control symbols. The mod writes no files of its own (DFHack notes its two widgets in dfhack-config/overlay.json) and does not use the network. It stores each chair's facing and position in your save as a small DFHack note.

All messages go to the DFHack console. If chairs do not change, look there for lines starting with improved-chairs: and run [code]overlay list improved-chairs[/code] to check that both widgets are enabled.

[h2]Removing it[/h2]
Unsubscribe. Also delete any copy of this mod left under mods and data/installed_mods in the game's data folder; the game keeps its own copy there. Every chair then looks as it did before. To switch the chairs off without removing the mod, run [code]overlay disable improved-chairs.map[/code] in the DFHack console.

[h2]Coming from version 1.0[/h2]
Version 1.0 copied a compiled plugin into DFHack's folder. Nothing uses it any more. With the game closed, delete these two files from the folder that contains DFHack's hack folder (in Steam: DFHack, Manage, Browse local files):
[code]hack/plugins/improved-chairs.plug.dll
hack/lua/plugins/improved-chairs.lua[/code]
If you installed 1.0 by hand from its archive, also delete these three, or the old script is used in place of this mod and no chair changes:
[code]hack/scripts/improved-chairs.lua
hack/data/art/chair_slat_template.png
hack/data/art/chair_controls.png[/code]
Your saved facings and positions are kept.

[h2]Installing by hand instead[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Download the archive from REPLACE_WITH_RELEASE_URL
[*]Extract it into the Dwarf Fortress folder, the one that contains dfhack-config. It adds three files and a licence text:
[code]dfhack-config/scripts/improved-chairs.lua
dfhack-config/scripts/internal/improved-chairs/template.lua
dfhack-config/scripts/internal/improved-chairs/chair_controls.png[/code]
[*]Start the game through DFHack. Nothing needs enabling.
[/olist]
To remove a hand install, delete those files. Use one way or the other, not both.

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Fortress mode with the graphical tiles only.
[*]Only built chairs change. Chairs in stockpiles or being hauled keep the game's picture.
[*]Moving a chair moves only its picture. A dwarf using it is still drawn in the middle of the tile.
[/list]

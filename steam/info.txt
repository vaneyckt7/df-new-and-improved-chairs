[STEAM_TITLE:New and Improved Chairs (rotate and move, DFHack)]
[STEAM_DESCRIPTION:Replaces built chairs with a heavy dwarven slat-back chair coloured from its material, and lets you turn each one to face north, east, south or west and slide it up to its table. Purely cosmetic. Needs DFHack. The mod is a plain DFHack script: it has no compiled plugin and writes no files outside its own folder. Tested on Dwarf Fortress 53.16 with DFHack 53.16-r1.1.]

New and Improved Chairs: the game's own chair pictures, for worlds generated with this mod.
Same names as the game's (vanilla_items_graphics/graphics/graphics_items.txt), so these take
their place.  Every style variant is the same chair; the leg variants are empty, because the
legs are part of the chair.  The last line is the build menu's Chair entry.  Made by art/make_sheet.py.
